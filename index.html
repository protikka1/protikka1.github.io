Client Case Studies

Case 1: Resolving E-Commerce Spam Placement & Conflicting SPF

Challenge: Client transactional receipts were routed to spam due to multiple conflicting SPF strings on Squarespace DNS breaking Google's 10-lookup threshold.

Solution: Consolidated SPF records into a single optimized syntax, published a 2048-bit google._domainkey DKIM selector, and enforced a phased DMARC policy.

Outcome: 100% primary inbox delivery restored with zero bounce rate.

Case 2: Secondary Domain Outreach Isolation

Challenge: An agency required secondary outbound sending domains to scale outreach without endangering the primary domain's reputation.

Solution: Set up domain routing inside Google Workspace, deployed isolated SPF/DKIM records across Cloudflare DNS, and configured SSL-secured Custom Tracking Domains (CNAME).

Outcome: Achieved 4/4 passing audit scores while keeping the primary corporate domain completely isolated.
